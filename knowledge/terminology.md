# Editable market and terminology guide

Create an entry for each target locale. Supply approved examples and sources;
an unanswered question is not permission to invent a market rule.

## Locale context

- Language and region, approved source language and variant mapping.
- Audience, formality, spelling and approved voice examples.
- Date, number, unit and currency conventions.
- Intentional local differences in content, offers and next actions.
- Market-specific product facts and qualifications, with approved sources.
- Local link destinations, shared/fallback pages and known exceptions.
- Shared CMS fields or invariant content, where verified.
- Reviewer, last review date and unresolved decisions.

## Term entries

For each important term record the source term, approved target term, locale,
context, names that remain untranslated, discouraged alternatives, a usage
example and the approving source. Record conflicts rather than choosing a
preferred term without evidence.

Link to maintained product facts instead of copying them into every locale.
Translation does not establish local availability or legal applicability.
