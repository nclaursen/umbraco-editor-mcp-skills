# Editable tone of voice

This is a questionnaire and example structure, not an established house style.
Replace the questions with your answers. The assistant should flag unanswered
items that materially affect the task rather than assume a brand personality.

## Reader and relationship

Who are we talking to? What do they already know? Should we sound like a
practitioner, adviser, peer, teacher or another role? What language and
regional spelling does this project use?

## Voice choices

How direct, formal and technical should the writing be? When should it use
first person? Where is humour helpful or inappropriate? Which terms need
explaining? Give examples instead of relying only on adjectives such as
friendly, bold or professional.

## Approved examples

Add two or three short passages from your own approved work. Identify the
source and explain which choices the assistant should learn from each.
Use examples for the intended audience; do not blend unrelated brands.

## Before and after

Illustrative example to replace with your own:

- Avoid: Our innovative solution empowers seamless transformation.
- Prefer: Export the report, choose one page and check what its current copy answers.
- Reason: Specific actions give this reader something useful to do.

## Boundaries

Which phrases should be avoided, and why? What editorial choices are
deliberate? When must a technical term, qualification or legal wording be
preserved? Voice never authorizes changing a supported factual claim.

## Maintenance

Record the owner, approved sources and last review date when you tailor
this file. Update the examples when the desired voice changes.
