# Editable business and content priorities

Answer these for the planning horizon relevant to your request:

- What offer, service or information does the site exist to support?
- Who is the audience and which outcomes matter?
- What is outside scope or deliberately not a priority?
- Which outcomes are measurable, with definitions in measurement.md?
- What editorial capacity, budget, owners and approval dependencies exist?
- Which markets, languages and dates does the plan cover?
- Which goals or targets are agreed, and which are only hypotheses?

Record sources and a review date. Missing targets remain unknown rather
than becoming invented benchmarks.
