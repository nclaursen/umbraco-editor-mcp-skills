# Editable evidence register

Create one entry for each material claim you want the assistant to reuse.

For each entry record:

- The exact supported fact and its product/version or other scope.
- Primary source URL or supplied file and relevant passage.
- Owner, verification date and when it needs checking again.
- Required qualifications, wording restrictions or approval dependencies.
- Status: verified, supplied but unverified, expired or awaiting approval.

An empty register does not mean all claims are permitted. Have the assistant
retrieve authorized sources or identify the missing evidence. Do not store
credentials, private customer details or secrets here.
