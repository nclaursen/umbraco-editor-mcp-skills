# Editable audience context

Replace these prompts with facts for your project. Keep guesses labelled.

- Primary reader, role and relevant level of expertise.
- Task or decision that brings them to this page.
- Questions, objections and constraints, with their evidence sources.
- What the page should help them do next.
- Language, geography and accessibility needs relevant to the brief.
- Secondary audiences and any conflicting needs.
- Sources, owner and last review date.

Do not invent buyer research, interviews or demand to fill unanswered items.
