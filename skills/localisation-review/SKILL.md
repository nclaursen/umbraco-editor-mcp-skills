---
name: localisation-review
description: Compare source and target language pages for meaning, completeness, terminology, local links and actions using approved market guidance.
---

Read the source and target variants, audience.md, voice.md, approved-facts.md
and terminology.md when supplied. Establish which language is the approved
source, the target locale and whether the task is review or revision. Do not
assume that English is the source or that matching slugs identify equivalents.
Use verified variant relationships, supplied mappings or available CMS tools.

Compare the page promise, sections, blocks, factual qualifications, media,
metadata and CTAs. Match their editorial purpose rather than demanding equal
word counts or block order. Record missing material, changed meaning and
intentional local differences supported by the brief.

Apply approved terms, names, spelling, formality and locale conventions.
Check dates, numbers, units and currencies without inventing exchange rates
or local offers. Product availability, prices, legal statements and support
commitments require approved market-specific sources; translation alone does
not establish that a claim applies in another market.

Inspect links and action destinations for the intended locale and published
status where possible. Distinguish language variants from fallback content
and do not call shared/invariant fields missing translations. For CMS writes,
verify the requested culture and field's variant/invariant behaviour first.
Unavailable variant information is a stated limitation, not a guessed mapping.

Return findings with the source passage, target field/passage, issue, proposed
wording, approved terminology/source and human decisions. Prioritise changed
meaning or unsupported local claims above stylistic preferences. If the
language or domain exceeds what you can reliably assess, mark the passages
for a qualified reviewer rather than claiming a complete language sign-off.

Review does not authorize CMS writes. When revision is requested, preserve
supported local adaptations and change only the agreed variant and fields.
Report shared-field effects and do not overwrite other cultures or publish
without explicit authorization.
