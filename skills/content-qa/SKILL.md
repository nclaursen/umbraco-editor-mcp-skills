description: Review complete page drafts for editorial quality and publication hygiene including links, heading structure, image alt text, metadata and component constraints.

Infer review-only, fix or final-verification mode from the user request.
Review-only does not authorize editing. Read the complete draft, accepted
brief and relevant audience, voice, approved-facts and measurement knowledge.
For component-based pages also read the verified page-blocks catalogue.

Check that the page promise, audience, argument and next action align.
Identify repetition, missing reasoning, unclear terminology, unsupported
claims, inaccurate counts and examples that fail to support the point.
Preserve the author's deliberate choices and useful personality.

Verify material claims against supplied evidence or current primary
sources. Separate facts, experience, hypotheses and opinions. Check dates,
units, denominators, comparison scopes and causal language. Do not invent
quotes, customers, outcomes or platform capabilities to improve the copy.

Compare the voice with actual examples in voice.md, respecting the language
and audience in the brief. Avoid treating a particular sentence pattern
as proof of AI authorship. Repair synthetic repetition when it harms
clarity, without flattening every sentence into the same style.

## Publication hygiene

Inspect the complete artifact, including rich text, component fields and
embedded media. Use the rendered page or preview where available; a Markdown
file alone cannot establish the final CMS heading structure or layout.

- **Links:** inventory every internal and external link, including buttons,
  linked images, media and source citations. Open destinations or check them
  through available tools. Check redirects, the destination's relevance and
  fragment targets. Resolve relative links against the intended publication
  URL; draft-folder resolution alone is insufficient. Distinguish a broken
  destination from authentication, bot protection or an unavailable checker.
  HTTP 200 alone does not establish the intended page or valid anchor. Use
  descriptive link text and verify CMS content-picker links resolve to their
  intended published pages when that evidence is available.
- **Headings:** verify one page-level H1 and a logical H2-H3 hierarchy without
  skipped levels. Include headings generated by blocks and templates, not
  just those typed in rich text. Check descriptive section labels and remove
  formatting-only headings. Preserve a deliberate hierarchy when justified
  by the brief rather than enforcing an arbitrary heading count.
- **Images and alt text:** inventory Markdown images, HTML images and CMS
  media fields. Check assets resolve and meaningful images have accurate,
  contextual alt text. Decorative images may have intentionally empty alt;
  do not invent descriptions or keyword-stuff them. A CMS field is not proof
  the template emits it. Verify rendered alt attributes when available and
  label field-only checks accordingly. If there are no images, report this
  check as not applicable rather than claiming alt text passed.
- **Other media:** check embeds and media sources where accessible, meaningful
  titles, captions/transcripts when needed, and broken or placeholder assets.
  Do not describe unviewed media or claim unavailable accessibility checks.
- **Structure and rendering:** inspect lists, tables, code fences, emphasis,
  quotations and HTML/Markdown syntax. In a preview check text overflow,
  clipping, mobile layout, legibility and whether component headings, links
  and media render as intended. Validate verified field lengths, required
  properties, nesting and block item limits against the site's knowledge.
- **Metadata:** check applicable title, description, canonical, slug, language,
  author, dates, social-image fields and frontmatter/schema against the
  publication system and brief. Keep dates truthful and preserve existing
  URLs unless a change is authorized. Distinguish metadata supplied by the
  site template from missing page fields.
- **Final-copy hygiene:** find unresolved editorial notes, accidental local
  paths, placeholders and draft alternatives. Distinguish intentional
  teaching examples from unfinished publication content.

For each failed or unavailable check, identify the affected field, asset or
destination, observed evidence and smallest corrective action. Report checks
as passed, failed, not applicable or unverified. Do not turn unavailable
tools into a passed check or imply preview validation that did not run.

In fix mode, resolve material issues and keep the diff within the brief.
Re-read after edits. Run the relevant build or validation when modifying
implementation files and supported by the target project. A prose-only
draft need not trigger an unrelated site build.

Lead with Ready, Ready after minor fixes or Not ready, scoped to the actual
deliverable. Include a concise publication-hygiene check summary alongside
material repairs, publication blockers and user decisions. Do not claim readiness when
a necessary source or check is unresolved. Stop once the brief is met.
Publication and external changes remain separate authorized actions.
