---
name: content-qa
description: Perform final review or authorized repairs on a complete page draft against its brief, evidence, voice and publication constraints.
---

Infer review-only, fix or final-verification mode from the user request.
Review-only does not authorize editing. Read the complete draft, accepted
brief and relevant audience, voice, approved-facts and measurement knowledge.
For component-based pages also read the verified page-blocks catalogue.

Check that the page promise, audience, argument and next action align.
Identify repetition, missing reasoning, unclear terminology, unsupported
claims, inaccurate counts and examples that fail to support the point.
Preserve the author's deliberate choices and useful personality.

Verify material claims against supplied evidence or current primary
sources. Separate facts, experience, hypotheses and opinions. Check dates,
units, denominators, comparison scopes and causal language. Do not invent
quotes, customers, outcomes or platform capabilities to improve the copy.

Compare the voice with actual examples in voice.md, respecting the language
and audience in the brief. Avoid treating a particular sentence pattern
as proof of AI authorship. Repair synthetic repetition when it harms
clarity, without flattening every sentence into the same style.

Check headings, links, metadata, visible content and requested CMS fields
using the evidence available. Validate known block limits and media needs.
Inspect the rendered preview when available; if absent, do not claim layout
was verified. Distinguish illustrative template prompts from accidental
placeholders left in final copy.

In fix mode, resolve material issues and keep the diff within the brief.
Re-read after edits. Run the relevant build or validation when modifying
implementation files and supported by the target project. A prose-only
draft need not trigger an unrelated site build.

Lead with Ready, Ready after minor fixes or Not ready, scoped to the actual
deliverable. List only material repairs, publication blockers, user
decisions and checks performed or unavailable. Do not claim readiness when
a necessary source or check is unresolved. Stop once the brief is met.
Publication and external changes remain separate authorized actions.
