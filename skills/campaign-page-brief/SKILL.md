---
name: campaign-page-brief
description: Turn an audience, campaign offer and goal into an evidence-backed page brief mapped to the site's available components before drafting.
---

Read the campaign inputs and relevant audience.md, business-goals.md,
approved-facts.md, voice.md, content-map.md and reviewed page-blocks.md.
Establish the audience's starting context, traffic source, offer, intended
next action, language, campaign dates and delivery constraints. Ask only for
missing decisions that materially affect the brief; keep other gaps visible.

State the page's promise and primary action. Identify the reader questions
and objections the page must resolve, and the approved evidence needed for
each. Distinguish supplied facts, proposed hypotheses and missing proof.
Do not invent testimonials, urgency, discounts or outcomes.

Check whether an existing page can serve the campaign before proposing a new
one. Recommend one page role and explain any overlap. Do not invent new
URLs, forms, downloads, tracking events or backend capabilities as if they
already exist. List those as dependencies with a suggested owner when known.

Build a section brief connecting each section's reader need, message, proof
and action to a verified available component and its fields. Use the reviewed
block catalogue or supplied schemas; if neither exists, make a content-only
brief and identify the component mapping as unresolved. Observed copy lengths
are guidance, not schema limits. Include media, alt-text and link needs.

Define success using agreed outcomes and measurement.md when available.
Separate the intended metric from tracking readiness and any proposed test.
Do not turn a target into a predicted result or require analytics to create
a useful editorial brief.

Return the brief, component/field mapping where supported, required assets,
sources, acceptance criteria and decisions needed before drafting. Use the
accepted brief for a later drafting task; the brief alone does not authorize
CMS creation, campaign launch, form configuration or publication.
